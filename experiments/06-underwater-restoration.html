Dr. ThomasStephan

software architect · team lead · computer vision

My doctorate was on restoring underwater images — modelling what happens to light on its way from a scene to a sensor when the medium in between refuses to cooperate. Scattering, absorption, veiling light: the image you get is not the image that was there, and getting it back is an inverse problem.

Since 2016 I write C++/Qt for molecular beam epitaxy systems, where a wrong number does not throw an exception — it grows the wrong crystal. Almost every expensive bug I have chased was never really a bug. I want that class of mistake to be unrepresentable: the meaning of a number encoded in its type, so the compiler objects on the line where it happens.
