Dr. Thomas Stephan

software architect · team lead · computer vision

I write the software that runs molecular beam epitaxy systems — machines that grow crystals one atomic layer at a time in a vacuum cleaner than deep space. C++/Qt, since 2016, team lead since 2020. Down here a wrong number does not throw an exception. It grows the wrong crystal, and you find out hours later when the run is already scrap.

Before that I did a doctorate on restoring underwater images: modelling what light does on its way from a scene to a sensor when the medium in between refuses to cooperate.

Almost every expensive bug I have chased in either world was never really a bug. A channel order nobody wrote down. A gamma curve everyone assumed. I want that class of mistake to be unrepresentable — the meaning of a number encoded in its type, so the compiler objects on the line where it happens.
