What I am actually after

My doctorate was on restoring underwater images — modelling what light does on its way from a scene to a sensor when the medium in between refuses to cooperate. Since 2016 I write C++/Qt for molecular beam epitaxy systems: machines where a wrong number does not throw an exception, it grows the wrong crystal.

Almost every expensive bug I have chased was never really a bug. A channel order nobody wrote down. A gamma curve everyone assumed. A pyramid level that drifted half a pixel and nobody noticed for a release.

I want that whole class of mistake to be unrepresentable — the meaning of a number encoded in its type, so the compiler objects on the line where it happens.
