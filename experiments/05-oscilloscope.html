My doctorate was on restoring underwater images: modelling what light does on its way from a scene to a sensor when the medium in between refuses to cooperate. Signal processing, Fourier analysis, nonlinear optimisation, information fusion — the handle comes from the lab I did it in.

Since 2016 I write C++/Qt for molecular beam epitaxy systems, where a wrong number does not throw an exception — it grows the wrong crystal. Almost every expensive bug I have chased was never really a bug. I want that class of mistake to be unrepresentable: the meaning of a number encoded in its type, so the compiler objects on the line where it happens.
