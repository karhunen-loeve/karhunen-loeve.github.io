Dr. Thomas Stephan

software architect · team lead · computer vision

I started out on multi-target tracking: probabilistic dynamic systems, and what to do when two targets are close enough that the sensor returns one detection instead of two. My diploma thesis was on exactly that — the probabilistic handling of merged detections.

My doctorate went to underwater image restoration: modelling what light does on its way from a scene to a sensor when the medium in between refuses to cooperate. Since 2016 I write C++/Qt for molecular beam epitaxy systems, where a wrong number does not throw an exception — it grows the wrong crystal.

Almost every expensive bug I have chased was never really a bug. I want that class of mistake to be unrepresentable: the meaning of a number encoded in its type, so the compiler objects on the line where it happens.
