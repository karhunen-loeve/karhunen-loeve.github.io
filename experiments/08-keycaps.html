Dr.-Ing. · software architect · team lead · computer vision

My doctorate was on restoring underwater images — modelling what light does on its way from a scene to a sensor when the medium in between refuses to cooperate. Since 2016 I write C++/Qt for molecular beam epitaxy systems, where a wrong number does not throw an exception, it grows the wrong crystal.

Almost every expensive bug I have chased was never really a bug: a channel order nobody wrote down, a gamma curve everyone assumed, a pyramid level that drifted half a pixel. I want that class of mistake to be unrepresentable — the meaning of a number encoded in its type, so the compiler objects on the line where it happens.
